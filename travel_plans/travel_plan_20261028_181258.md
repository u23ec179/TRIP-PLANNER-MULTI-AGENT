## 3️⃣ Day‑by‑Day Itinerary  

*(All times are suggestions; feel free to shift based on your flight arrival/departure.)*  

### **Day 1 – Arrival & Westminster Intro**  
- **Morning:** Land at Heathrow → take the **Heathrow Express** (₹2 500) or **Piccadilly Line** (₹300) to Central London.  
- **Check‑in** at hotel, drop luggage, freshen up.  
- **Lunch:** Grab a classic fish‑and‑chips at **Poppies** (Covent Garden).  
- **Afternoon:** Walk along the **South Bank** – see the **London Eye**, **Big Ben**, **Westminster Abbey** (exterior).  
- **Evening:** Early dinner at **The Red Lion** (traditional pub) and a leisurely stroll along the Thames.  

### **Day 2 – Museum Day (Free Attractions)**  
- **Breakfast:** Hotel (usually included).  
- **Morning:** **British Museum** (free) – see the Rosetta Stone, Egyptian mummies.  
- **Lunch:** Street food at **Borough Market** (₹1 200).  
- **Afternoon:** **National Gallery** (free) in Trafalgar Square, then **National Portrait Gallery** next door.  
- **Evening:** Optional West End show (tickets from ₹3 500 via TKTS).  

### **Day 3 – Tower of London & River Cruise**  
- **Morning:** Pre‑book **Tower of London** tickets (₹2 500 each). Arrive early to avoid crowds.  
- **Lunch:** Grab a bite at **St. Katharine Docks** (₹1 500).  
- **Afternoon:** **Thames River Cruise** from Tower Bridge to Westminster (₹1 200 each).  
- **Evening:** Walk through **St. James’s Park**, dinner at **The Ivy Café** (₹3 000).  

### **Day 4 – Day‑Trip to Windsor** *(Full‑day excursion)*  
- **Travel:** Take a **South Western Railway** train from London Waterloo to Windsor & Eton Central (₹2 000 round‑trip per person).  
- **Morning:** Tour **Windsor Castle** (₹2 800).  
- **Lunch:** Picnic in **Windsor Great Park** (packed from a local bakery, ₹800).  
- **Afternoon:** Explore **Eton College** and the town centre.  
- **Return:** Evening back in London, light dinner near the hotel.  

### **Day 5 – Camden & Regent’s Park**  
- **Morning:** Take the **Northern Line** to **Camden Town**. Browse the **Camden Market**, try a **Vegan Street Taco** (₹600).  
- **Mid‑day:** Walk along the **Regent’s Canal** to **Primrose Hill** for panoramic city views.  
- **Lunch:** Picnic in **Regent’s Park** (₹1 000).  
- **Afternoon:** Visit **London Zoo** (₹2 200 each) – optional; otherwise, relax in the park.  
- **Evening:** Dinner at **The Hawley Arms** (₹2 500).  

### **Day 6 – Royal London – Kensington & Hyde Park**  
- **Morning:** **Kensington Palace** (₹2 300).  
- **Lunch:** Café inside the **Victoria & Albert Museum** (₹1 500).  
- **Afternoon:** Free entry to **V&A Museum** and **Science Museum** – both excellent and free.  
- **Late Afternoon:** Stroll through **Hyde Park**, rent a paddle boat on the Serpentine (₹600).  
- **Evening:** Treat yourself to a **Afternoon Tea** at **The Orangery** (₹4 000).  

### **Day 7 – Last‑Minute Shopping & Departure**  
- **Morning:** Check‑out (store luggage at hotel).  
- **Shop:** **Oxford Street** & **Regent Street** – pick up souvenirs, maybe a **London-themed scarf** (₹2 000).  
- **Lunch:** Quick bite at **Pret A Manger** (₹800).  
- **Afternoon:** Retrieve luggage, head to Heathrow via **Heathrow Express** or **Tube**.  
- **Evening:** Flight back home.
